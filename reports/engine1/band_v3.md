# Engine 1 band candidate R3: monthly clip, population record, refit (engine v3)

> Generated by `python -m ml.engine1_eval.band_v3` - do not edit by hand. The groups, metrics and the serve rule were written in the team's private planning notes before this ran.

Each row is a point in a synthetic student's history (origins from week 17 every 4 weeks), 300 resampled 13-week totals per rule, and the share of points whose realised total fell inside the simulated P10-P90 (*inside*, should be 0.80). *Median bias* is the median of (simulated median - realised) / (recent level x 13): above 0 = too high.

- **x1.5 (R1)**: the user's own error record widened by 1.5, every simulated week floored at zero (engine v1).
- **served table (R2)**: the D-54 table, weekly floor (served before this round).
- **candidate (R3)**: each calendar month's total floored at zero instead of each week (D-60); below 26 weeks of history a population record of 13-week ratio error paths times the user's recent level (D-61); the table refitted on the development students under both.
- **MA13, R3 (benchmark)**: the 13-week moving average through the same R3 method, with its own population record and table.

## Decision (plan §4.4)

1. overall coverage moves toward 0.80 for both kinds: **holds**.
2. |median bias| shrinks for both kinds: **holds**.
3. no kind x bucket cell below min(R2 - 0.05, 0.80): **holds**.
4. outcome-check Brier R3 - R2: 95% interval not entirely above 0, both cuts: **holds**.
   Outcome check, cut 13 weeks: Brier R3 - R2 -0.0018 (95% interval -0.0034 to -0.0004).
   Outcome check, cut 26 weeks: Brier R3 - R2 -0.0053 (95% interval -0.0073 to -0.0032).

**R3 is served.**

## Limits

- Synthetic students only; MoneyData and Berka cannot be downloaded in this environment, so the real-data check of R3 is a local step.
- The cohort holds ALL income; serving forecasts only income not covered by a confirmed recurring item (note N2). Income coverage here measures a series serving mostly places as known flows.
- Short histories in D and F start at the cohort's first week, so the 17-25 bucket is scored in one season; the population record's windows are spread across the year.
