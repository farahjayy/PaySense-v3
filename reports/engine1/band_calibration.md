# Band calibration: widening the error record by history length (WP16, C2)

> Generated by `python -m ml.engine1_eval.band_calibration` - do not edit by hand.

Each row is a point in a synthetic student's history: what serving would build there (the user's own error paths, or the fallback when the record is too short), the served LSTM's forecast, and 300 resampled 13-week totals. *Inside P10-P90* is the share of points where the real total fell inside the band; it should be 0.80. v2 widened every record by 1.5; v3 uses the factor fitted for the history length and series.

## Limits

- Synthetic students only (data/synthetic/weekly_series.csv, regenerated by `ml.datagen.export`). MoneyData and Berka could not be downloaded in this environment (network policy), so no real-data coverage is claimed for v3; the v2 real-data rows stay in `serving_check.md` as the earlier version.
- The cohort is 78 weeks long, so the last bucket is fitted on 52-65 weeks of history and applied to anything longer.
- Coverage is of the 13-week total. P(shortfall) also depends on the centre of the band, which widening does not correct (the bias line above).
