# Taking part in a short study of a BNPL decision aid

## What this is

PaySense is a student project that helps people decide whether a "buy now, pay later" (BNPL) purchase is a good idea. It forecasts how your money is likely to look over the coming months and checks how a particular purchase would change that. We would like to see whether it is easy to use and whether its explanations make sense.

## What you will do

About 40 minutes with a facilitator:

1. Read three short everyday purchase scenarios and say what you would do.
2. Use PaySense on a **practice account**, doing a few simple tasks.
3. Answer the same scenarios again, then a few short questions about how it felt to use.

## Your data

- The practice account holds **made-up** transactions for an imaginary student. **You do not enter any of your own bank, e-wallet or BNPL details.** Please do not.
- We write down your answers and short notes under a participant code (for example P03). Your name is not written next to your answers.
- Answers are used only for this project's report, in aggregate. The report may quote a short comment without anything that identifies you.
- The practice account is kept on a hosted database in Singapore for the duration of the project. It contains no personal information about you.

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question and stop at any time without giving a reason, and nothing happens to you if you do.
- You can ask for your answers to be removed at any time before the report is written. Tell the facilitator your participant code.
- This is a test of the app, not of you. There are no right answers.

Ask the facilitator, or contact the project author at the address on the project's repository page.
