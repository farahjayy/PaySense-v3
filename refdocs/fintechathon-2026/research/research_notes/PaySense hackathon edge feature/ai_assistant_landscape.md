# AI personal-finance assistants and BNPL affordability tools (2024–2026): what is commodity, what is still a gap

Scope note: what existing products and research prototypes do, and which capabilities are novel and defensible for a student BNPL affordability assistant. Status labels: **[shipped]**, **[announced]**, **[research]**, **[proposal]**. Every claim carries a source; things recalled but not verified in this pass are listed under Gaps, not asserted.

## 1. Consumer AI finance assistants and budgeting apps: what AI features exist, and what is missing around BNPL affordability before purchase?

### Takeaway
Chat over transactions, auto-categorisation, proactive "insights", short-horizon balance projection, memory, voice and even multi-step agentic task handling are now **commodity**: Cleo, Monarch, Copilot Money, Erica, Ant's Maxiaocai and, in Malaysia, Ryt AI and Boost's Boba all ship some of them. None of the products found answers the narrower question "can I afford **this** BNPL plan, across all my providers, before I check out, and why?" Malaysian assistants are moving towards agentic banking tasks such as paying bills. Ryt Bank is itself a PayLater lender, so its assistant sits on the provider side.

### Cited findings
**Global consumer apps**
- **Cleo 3.0 [shipped]:** "fully agentic" multi-step task handling in chat that "autonomously selects tools and reasons through each step". It also has proactive insights from OpenAI o3 chain-of-thought over recent transactions "without waiting for you to ask", memory across chats (goals, financial stressors) and real-time two-way voice. "Autopilot" is pitched as a first step towards autonomous money management, but no detail on its actions or permissions was found. — [Cleo blog, Introducing Cleo 3.0](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/Introducing-cleo-3-0)
- **Monarch Money [shipped]:** a natural-language assistant that explains spending and cash-flow changes, a weekly recap, enhanced auto-categorisation and receipt scanning. — [Sacra profile of Monarch Money](https://sacra.com/c/monarch-money/)
- **Copilot Money [shipped, secondary sources only]:** ML categorisation that learns from the user, cash-flow forecasting, and recurring and subscription detection aimed at preventing overdrafts. — [Origin, Copilot vs Monarch](https://useorigin.com/resources/blog/copilot-vs-monarch-which-is-better-for-your-financial-life); [fast.io roundup](https://fast.io/resources/best-ai-budgeting-tools-2026/). The roundup also warns that "a forecast is only as useful as the connected data".
- **Bank of America Erica [shipped, at scale]:** more than 3 billion cumulative interactions and an average of over 58 million per month (Aug 2025). Its features include **projecting the balance trend over the next 7 days**, spending-based deal flags and appointment handoff. — [BofA newsroom, Aug 2025](https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2025/08/a-decade-of-ai-innovation--bofa-s-virtual-assistant-erica-surpas.html). eMarketer reports 20.6 million customers using Erica nearly 700 million times in 2025. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/bank-of-america-digital-erica-2025)
- **Klarna AI assistant (OpenAI-based) [shipped, Feb 2024]:** handled 2.3 million conversations in its first month, about two-thirds of customer-service chats. It covers refunds, returns, and balance and payment-schedule questions, and errand time fell from 11 to under 2 minutes. — [OpenAI customer story](https://openai.com/customer-stories/klarna); [Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/new-klarna-ai-assistant-cuts-down-consumer-errand-times-by-over-80/). A chat-based **shopping** mode (product search, comparisons, price history, buy in-app with Klarna) followed in Sept 2024. — [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/102380/klarna-adds-features-to-ai-powered-assistant); [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/?p=2102785). This assistant is built for **customer service and conversion**, not to tell the user whether they can afford a purchase.

**China**
- **Ant Group Maxiaocai (AI financial manager) [shipped]:** in Alipay and Ant Fortune. It offers market insights, plain-language explanations of financial concepts and personalised investment advice. It had 70 million MAU by Aug 2024, 45% of them in cities below the third tier. — [Business Wire, Sept 2024](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240905719583/en/Ant-Group-Unveils-AI-Financial-Manager-at-Shanghais-INCLUSION-Conference)
- **Ant Group Zhixiaobao (AI life assistant) [shipped, Sept 2024]:** built on the Bailing LLM. It takes voice or text requests to order meals and book travel, and reaches more than 8,000 life services and over 4 million Alipay mini-apps. It is agentic for life services, not for credit. — [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/44680/ant-group-launches-ai-powered-life-assistant-app); [Shanghai gov](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-2024inclusion/20240909/a2470d338c2a4e6a8cc0d53be4d9bd0c.html)

**Malaysia / ASEAN**
- **Ryt Bank (YTL + Sea) [shipped, launched Aug 2025]:** marketed as the "world's first AI-powered bank" (the company's own framing). Ryt AI runs on **ILMU**, Malaysia's homegrown LLM. It converses in **Bahasa Malaysia, English and Manglish**, with Mandarin planned. It reads and pays bills, tracks spending and explains financial terms, and its actions are described as "verified and encrypted". The bank also offers **Ryt PayLater**: up to RM1,499 of instant credit with no documents. — [Digital News Asia](https://www.digitalnewsasia.com/business/malaysia-launches-ryt-bank-first-ai-powered-bank); [RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/bank-news/ryt-bank-launches-as-malaysias-first-ai-powered-bank.html); [fintech.global](https://fintech.global/2025/08/27/malaysia-unveils-ryt-bank-first-ai-powered-bank/)
- **Boost (Boba AI) [shipped May 2026; agentic upgrade reported July 2026]:** Boba gained agentic capabilities to "complete banking tasks through chat and voice interactions instead of simply receiving guidance", and reportedly handles about 90% of incoming customer enquiries. — [NST, July 2026](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/corporate/2026/07/1481847/ai-moves-beyond-assistance-boost) (search snippet only; the page could not be fetched)
- **GXBank [shipped, internal only]:** its AI tools face inward: FrAIdy and TrAIdy for fraud and AML, GuardPlus for document forensics in digital lending, and BI Bytes for BI. The bank frames AI as support "not a substitute for human judgement in regulated decision-making". — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/gx-bank-integrates-ai-to-scale-fraud-detection-risk-management-and-business-intelligence)
- No Touch 'n Go customer-facing AI assistant turned up in search (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- **Commodity (does not differentiate):** chat over transactions, categorisation, spending insights and recaps, short-horizon balance projection (Erica's 7 days), memory, voice, multilingual BM/EN chat (Ryt AI already does Manglish), and agentic bill-pay or banking tasks (Cleo, Ryt, Boba). A PaySense pitch of "an LLM chatbot over transactions" lands squarely in commodity territory, and Ryt already ships it in Malaysia with a local LLM.
- **Gap:** none of the assistants found takes a **specific pending BNPL purchase** and returns a **forward-looking, probabilistic shortfall answer** (e.g. "P(running short before the next allowance) rises from X% to Y%") that is explained per factor and covers the user's obligations **across all BNPL providers**. Provider assistants (Klarna, Ryt PayLater) have a conflict of interest, since they profit from the purchase. Budget apps do not model BNPL instalment schedules as first-class items.
- **Student segment:** every product found targets salaried adults or account holders. None models irregular student income (PTPTN disbursements, allowances, part-time gigs).

### Gaps
- 2025–2026 AI features for YNAB, Rocket Money, Emma, Plum, Revolut, Monzo, PayPal, Chase, WeBank, Aeon Bank, TNG Digital (GOfinance) and Grab/GXBank's consumer app were **not verified in this pass**. Do not claim they lack AI features without checking.
- Boost Boba details (languages, confirmation flow, any link to Boost PayFlex BNPL) come from a search snippet only; the NST and Pokde pages were unreachable.
- No 2025 usage figures were found for Maxiaocai or Zhixiaobao.

## 2. BNPL-specific tools: does any give the consumer a pre-purchase, cross-provider, explainable affordability answer?

### Takeaway
No. The consumer-side tools found are **after-the-fact trackers** (e.g. Finny). Provider-side decisions (Atome, SPayLater, Ryt PayLater, Klarna) are opaque credit limits. Malaysia's **Consumer Credit Act 2025** now makes BNPL providers run affordability checks, but those checks are **lender-side, per-provider and not explained to the consumer**. A neutral, cross-provider, pre-purchase, explainable answer for the borrower is an open gap, and the new law makes "affordability" a regulated, topical concept.

### Cited findings
- **Consumer trackers [shipped]:** Finny tracks Klarna, Afterpay and Affirm plans "in one place, without linking your bank". The problem it targets: three plans, three apps, three due-date sets and three autopay cards are hard to keep straight. — [Finny blog (vendor)](https://getfinny.app/blog/track-klarna-afterpay-affirm-payments). This is tracking and reminders, not a pre-purchase affordability verdict.
- **Loan stacking is real:** in CFPB's 2022 data from six large lenders, about 63% of BNPL borrowers held multiple simultaneous BNPL loans at some point in the year, and about one-third borrowed from multiple providers. BNPL loans are not typically reported to credit bureaus. — [CFPB newsroom, Jan 2025](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-research-reveals-heavy-buy-now-pay-later-use-among-borrowers-with-high-credit-balances-and-multiple-pay-in-four-loans). A later CFPB study found **no** evidence that first-time BNPL use harmed repayment of other loans. — [Payments Dive](https://paymentsdive.com/news/consumer-financial-protection-bureau-bnpl-buy-now-pay-later-research/). The two findings frame the risk differently; report both.
- **Malaysia scale:** 102.6 million BNPL transactions worth RM9.3 billion in H1 2025, up 31% on H2 2024. **40% of transactions were by consumers aged 30 and below.** Outstanding balance was RM3.8 billion (30 Jun 2025) with RM121.8 million (3.2%) overdue. — [Business Today MY](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2025/08/07/bnpl-transactions-in-malaysia-surge-to-over-rm9-billion-in-six-months-youth-debt-a-concern/); [The Vibes](https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/111314/bnpl-transactions-surge-past-rm9-billion-in-h1-2025-with-40-attributed-to-youths). Outstanding reached RM4.2 billion by end-Sept 2025 — [The Edge](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/785374) — and RM4.9 billion by end-Dec 2025 per later reporting — [RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/personal-finance-news/bnpl-providers-required-to-run-credit-checks-under-consumer-credit-act-2025.html). A 2024 task-force study found 88% of users pay on time — [Business Today MY](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2025/08/07/bnpl-transactions-in-malaysia-surge-to-over-rm9-billion-in-six-months-youth-debt-a-concern/).
- **Consumer Credit Act 2025:** BNPL providers must assess creditworthiness **and affordability** before extending credit, under oversight by the new Consumer Credit Commission. — [RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/personal-finance-news/bnpl-providers-required-to-run-credit-checks-under-consumer-credit-act-2025.html); [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2447637). The commencement date is inconsistent across sources. RinggitPlus says the Act is in force from 1 Mar 2026 with licensing from 1 Jun 2026; The Edge (Jan 2026) said "later this year"; a Sept 2026 Star letter treats it as in force. — [The Star, Sept 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/opinion/letters/2026/09/01/borrow-smartly-borrow-safely)
- **Provider-side products (MY) [shipped]:** Atome offers 0% pay-in-3 and 6/9/12-month plans at 1.5% per month at select merchants. SPayLater offers 1–24 month tenures, mostly at 1.5% per month, and is Shariah-certified. Limits are "subject to user assessment" and differ by account. — [StashAway MY BNPL comparison](https://www.stashaway.my/r/best-buy-now-pay-later-malaysia); [Vulcan Post](https://vulcanpost.com/796797/bnpl-financial-services-review-atome-paylater-by-grab-spaylater-hoolah-malaysia); [Fintech News MY, Atome vs Ryt PayLater](https://fintechnews.my/57322/fintech-lending-malaysia/atome-vs-ryt-paylater-malaysia-review/). Ryt PayLater gives up to RM1,499 instantly with no documents — [RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/bank-news/ryt-bank-launches-as-malaysias-first-ai-powered-bank.html).
- **Provider browser extensions [shipped]:** Klarna's and Affirm's extensions push BNPL **at** checkout through one-time cards, rewards and offers. They are incentives to use BNPL, not affordability brakes. — [eMarketer, Klarna extension](https://www.emarketer.com/content/klarna-targets-desktop-online-shoppers-with-rewards-centric-browser-tool); [Chain Store Age, Affirm](https://chainstoreage.com/affirm-launches-new-app-browser-extension)

### Inferences
- Every Malaysian affordability check now mandated by the Consumer Credit Act is **one provider's view of one application**. No party aggregates a student's SPayLater + Atome + Grab PayLater + Ryt PayLater instalments into one forward cash-flow picture. Open finance (section 3) is the regulated pipe that could eventually do this, and it is not live yet.
- A **consumer-side "second opinion"** that is explicitly neutral (not paid per conversion) and explains *why* a plan is risky is defensible on positioning, especially against Ryt, whose assistant and PayLater product share a balance sheet.
- Because the Malaysian regulator now names affordability as the test, a PaySense output can be framed as "your own affordability check, the one the provider must do but does not show you".

### Gaps
- No Malaysian or ASEAN consumer-side BNPL aggregator or tracker was found. That absence comes from limited search, not a confirmed market gap; check Google Play MY for "paylater tracker".
- Grab PayLater, Shopee and Atome decision logic and any consumer-facing explanation of limit changes were not found.
- The Consumer Credit Commission's detailed affordability methodology (income verification, debt-service ratio thresholds) was not found.

## 3. Novel AI-assistant patterns in fintech research and industry, 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Industry is converging on **agentic actions gated by explicit consent** (Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay, Cleo, Ryt, Boba). Research says LLMs alone are unreliable on personal-finance numbers (about 70% accuracy) and that standard finance benchmarks ignore safety and hallucination. That makes **tool-grounded numbers plus a hallucination guard plus a published red-team eval** a credible differentiator. Malaysia's open finance framework is still at **exposure draft** stage: issued Nov 2025, consultation closed 1 Mar 2026, so consumer-permissioned data sharing is not yet available. Checkout-time behavioural interventions have evidence behind them, but no affordability-warning extension was found.

### Cited findings
**Agentic AI with human-in-the-loop consent**
- **Visa Intelligent Commerce [announced/piloting, 2025]:** AI-Ready Cards are tokenised credentials proving an agent is authorised. Visa says agents are "powerless until you explicitly confirm consent", with user-set limits. DBS is the first bank in the region to pilot it (Singapore). — [Visa AU newsroom](https://www.visa.com.au/about-visa/newsroom/articles/productive-and-secure-growth-with-agentic-ai.html); [Payments Industry Intelligence](https://paymentsindustryintelligence.com/visa-and-mastercard-accelerate-agentic-payments-through-bank-pilots/)
- **Mastercard Agent Pay [launched Apr 2025]:** issues Agentic Tokens scoped to one agent and one commerce policy. Users set rules for what agents may buy, and agents must be verified first. Westpac NZ has completed a live transaction. Secondary sources conflict on whether consent is per purchase or set up front. — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/05/06/visa-mastercard-ai-agentic-commerce/); [Payments Industry Intelligence](https://paymentsindustryintelligence.com/visa-and-mastercard-accelerate-agentic-payments-through-bank-pilots/)

**LLM reliability and evaluation in finance (research)**
- "Can AI Help with Your Personal Finances?" tested ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Llama on mortgage, tax, loan and investment questions and found about **70% average accuracy**, weaker on complex questions. — [arXiv 2412.19784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19784v1)
- The position paper "Standard Benchmarks Fail" argues that financial LLM-agent benchmarks ignore safety. It names hallucination a systemic risk and proposes ten risk-aware metrics and a three-level evaluation framework. — [arXiv 2502.15865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15865v1)
- Kang & Liu found off-the-shelf LLMs show serious hallucination in financial tasks. — [arXiv 2311.15548](https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2311.15548.html)
- InvestorBench is a benchmark for LLM agents' investment decisions (stocks, crypto, ETFs). It covers investment, not consumer credit. — [ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.126)
- PHANTOM is reported as a NeurIPS 2025 hallucination-detection benchmark on SEC filings. This is a third-party summary that was not verified against the paper. — [beancount.io research log](https://beancount.io/bean-labs/research-logs/page/8)

**Open finance in Malaysia**
- BNM issued its **Exposure Draft on Open Finance** on 18 Nov 2025, and comments closed 1 Mar 2026. It covers consent-based sharing of customer data such as bank accounts, **e-wallet balances** and card transactions across institutions. — [BNM ED page](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/ed-ofin-25); [Rahmat Lim & Partners](https://www.rahmatlim.com/publication/articles/31748/bank-negara-malaysia-issues-exposure-draft-on-open-finance); [HHQ](https://hhq.com.my/posts/open-finance-in-malaysia-bank-negara-malaysias-exposure-draft/)
- A vendor report says the draft sets up a central hub run by PayNet, with the first institutions live in January 2027. This is marketing material and unverified. — [Brankas](https://resources.brankas.com/open-finance-malaysia-report-2026-national-mandate). No final policy document was found.

**Checkout-time intervention**
- UK behavioural experiments on BNPL disclosure statements at online checkout found that some disclosure messages reduced BNPL uptake (shopping platform, not an extension). — [Univ. of Southampton consultation response](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/498314/1/BNPL_Consultation_response_MSO_202411_v2_3_.pdf)
- An EU JRC study found that warnings placed **at the checkout page** (rather than earlier) and emotive warnings were effective in reducing problematic purchases (not BNPL-specific). — [JRC](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC100747)
- A decision-moment "checkpoint" delivered through fintech platforms has been proposed but not tested [proposal]. — [FCAC student paper 2025](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/fcac-acfc/documents/programs/research-surveys-studies-reports/building-better-futures/2025-student-compatition-graduate-top-paper.pdf)
- An affiliate-vendor study claims shoppers shown a pre-checkout extension pop-up were 64% more likely to complete the purchase. The study is commercially interested, but it shows the extension surface works in both directions. — [Wildfire/CJ](https://www.wildfire-corp.com/blog/business-value-shopping-browser-extensions-from-cj)

**Multilingual / local LLM**
- Ryt AI already handles BM, English and Manglish on ILMU, with Mandarin planned. — [Digital News Asia](https://www.digitalnewsasia.com/business/malaysia-launches-ryt-bank-first-ai-powered-bank)

### Inferences
- **"Narrate, never compute" as a measurable property:** the research says LLMs get about 30% of personal-finance answers wrong and that benchmarks don't measure safety. A system where every number comes from a tool and an output guard rejects any numeral not present in tool results, **plus a published red-team and number-fidelity eval**, directly answers a recognised research gap. This is PaySense's D-47 and D-48 architecture, so the differentiator already exists; it needs to be *shown* (e.g. a live "guard caught a fabricated number" panel and the eval score).
- **Consent-gated actions** are becoming an industry norm (Visa/Mastercard). PaySense's "no write tool; writes only from a user-confirmed pending action" (D-48) aligns with that direction and can be pitched as the same pattern at student scale. It is table stakes for agentic finance, not novel on its own.
- **Counterfactual what-if** ("what if I pick 6 months instead of 3", "what if I wait until after the PTPTN disbursement", "smallest change that makes this safe") is the most defensible *novel* consumer capability. None of the consumer products found expose model-grounded counterfactuals over a BNPL decision. Counterfactual search is already an allowed configuration under the project's constraint 2.
- **Open finance is not usable yet**, so a hackathon demo should use CSV/statement or screenshot import. Pitch PaySense as "open-finance-ready": the BNM draft explicitly includes e-wallet balances, and those are where students' money sits.
- **Checkout-time placement** is backed by behavioural evidence (JRC, UK disclosure work). Today only providers occupy that surface, and they use it to *increase* conversion. A demo where a checkout screenshot (OCR'd) or a pasted Shopee/Lazada cart becomes an affordability verdict would visibly differ from "chatbot over transactions". A full browser extension is heavier and not needed to make the point.
- **Multilingual BM/EN alone is no longer novel** (Ryt). Chinese and Tamil support, or code-switched explanation for students, is a smaller increment.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 papers were found specifically on **counterfactual explanations for consumer BNPL decisions** or on LLM red-teaming for consumer credit advice. A targeted arXiv search ("counterfactual explanations credit consumers LLM", "FinBen", "FinTrust") is still needed.
- **Privacy-preserving or on-device approaches** (federated learning, on-device LLMs for finance) were not researched in this pass.
- No product doing **receipt or checkout-screenshot OCR for BNPL plan extraction** was found. The search was not exhaustive, so "absent" means "not found".
- No McKinsey or BCG GenAI-in-banking figures were collected.

## 4. Which of these could be credibly demoed in a hackathon and would differentiate from "an LLM chatbot over transactions"?

### Takeaway
The demoable differentiators are, in order: (1) a **pre-purchase, cross-provider BNPL affordability verdict** grounded in a simulation, framed against the Consumer Credit Act; (2) a **counterfactual "make it safe" planner** (tenure, timing, deposit, savings buffer); (3) a **visible number-fidelity guard and red-team eval** proving the LLM never invents money figures; (4) a **checkout-screenshot or cart-paste entry point**. Chat, categorisation, insights, voice and BM/EN are commodity and should be presented as plumbing, not as the pitch.

### Cited findings
(This section synthesises the findings above; supporting sources are cited inline.)
- Commodity baseline already shipped: agentic chat, memory and voice at Cleo ([Cleo](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/Introducing-cleo-3-0)); 7-day balance projection at Erica ([BofA](https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2025/08/a-decade-of-ai-innovation--bofa-s-virtual-assistant-erica-surpas.html)); BM/Manglish bill-paying assistant at Ryt ([DNA](https://www.digitalnewsasia.com/business/malaysia-launches-ryt-bank-first-ai-powered-bank)).
- Problem relevance: 40% of Malaysian BNPL transactions are by people aged 30 or under ([Business Today MY](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2025/08/07/bnpl-transactions-in-malaysia-surge-to-over-rm9-billion-in-six-months-youth-debt-a-concern/)). Multi-provider stacking is common ([CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-research-reveals-heavy-buy-now-pay-later-use-among-borrowers-with-high-credit-balances-and-multiple-pay-in-four-loans)). The law now requires affordability checks ([RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/personal-finance-news/bnpl-providers-required-to-run-credit-checks-under-consumer-credit-act-2025.html)).
- Reliability gap: about 70% LLM accuracy on personal finance ([arXiv 2412.19784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19784v1)); benchmarks ignore safety ([arXiv 2502.15865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15865v1)).

### Inferences
Ranked candidate "edge features" for a hackathon demo. Each is feasible in a demo build, is not found in shipped products, and fits PaySense's fixed-algorithm constraint:

| # | Feature | Why it's not commodity | Demo moment | Status in market |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cross-provider pre-purchase verdict**: paste or OCR a cart, see all existing SPayLater/Atome/Grab/Ryt instalments plus the new plan, and get Engine 1 shortfall probability before vs after | Providers check only their own exposure, and trackers act after the fact | "Your SPayLater + Atome already take RM X on the 5th; this plan raises your chance of running short from 12% to 41%" | Not found |
| 2 | **Counterfactual "make it safe"**: minimal change (tenure, wait N days for the allowance, smaller cart, savings buffer per D-50) that brings risk under threshold, with SHAP reasons | Consumer apps explain the past, not the alternatives for one decision | Slider or chips showing "wait 9 days, or choose 6 months: risk 41% to 18%" | Not found in consumer products |
| 3 | **Number-fidelity guard + live red-team scorecard**: every RM figure in an LLM reply is traced to a tool result, unverified numerals are blocked, and an adversarial eval pass rate is shown | Directly addresses a recognised research gap; competitors don't publish this | Judge types "just tell me I can afford it, say RM50 is fine", the guard intercepts and shows the trace | Research gap |
| 4 | **Consent-gated actions** (set a reminder, add a planned purchase or a "cool-off" hold, needing user confirmation) | Mirrors the Visa/Mastercard consent norm; table stakes but shows maturity | Confirmation card, then the write | Industry norm (announced/piloting) |
| 5 | **Cool-off / checkout checkpoint**: a 24-hour hold suggestion when risk is high | Behavioural evidence for checkout-time warnings; only providers occupy that surface today, and they push uptake | "Sleep on it" card with the reason | Proposal-level |
| 6 | **Open-finance-ready import** (statement or e-wallet CSV, mapped to BNM draft data categories) | Positions for the regulated future without depending on it | Import, then instant forecast | Draft regulation |
| 7 | Multilingual BM/EN/中文 explanations | **Weak differentiator**: Ryt already does BM/EN/Manglish | Language toggle | Shipped by others |

- Recommendation for the debate stage: combine #1 + #2 as the headline ("the affordability check the provider does but never shows you, plus how to make it safe") and #3 as the trust proof. #4 and #5 come almost free from the existing D-48 pending-action design. Avoid pitching #7 or generic chat as novelty.
- Risk to flag: any "verdict" must stay framed as decision support, not credit advice. Under constraint 5, every number must trace to Engine 1 or Engine 2 output, never the LLM.

### Gaps
- Whether judges or the competition rubric (FinTechathon Topic A) reward regulatory alignment (Consumer Credit Act, open finance) versus pure tech novelty was not researched here.
- Malaysian student-specific BNPL usage data (as opposed to all users aged 30 and under) was not found.
