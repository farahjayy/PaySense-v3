# Registration summary (draft)

> Target 100–200 words. Figures from `reports/engine1/README.md`, `reports/engine2/versions.md` and `reports/assistant/README.md`; re-check them before submitting.

PaySense helps Malaysian university students decide whether a buy-now-pay-later plan fits before they commit. From a student's own records it forecasts the months ahead (a global LSTM, chosen over ARIMA, Prophet and simple benchmarks with a pre-registered rule; MASE 0.82 against 1.04 for the best benchmark), simulates a thousand futures, and scores a proposed SPayLater, TikTok PayLater or Atome plan as Safe, Caution or At Risk with a calibrated LightGBM model, its reasons, and the smallest change that would make it Safe. An AI assistant answers in plain words by calling the app's own tools: it never produces a figure itself (every number it says is checked against the tools' results), and it cannot change anything; it proposes, and the student confirms. In a live evaluation it completed 36 of 36 tasks. The model is trained on synthetic students, not real repayments, and we say so: its limits are published beside its results. PaySense gives estimates, not financial advice or a credit decision.
