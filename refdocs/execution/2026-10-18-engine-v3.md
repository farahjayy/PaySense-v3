## Deviations from the plan
- The new fresh check and the R3 fit share one generated page, `reports/engine1/band_v3.md` (the plan named two files); the earlier `band_fresh_check.md` stays as the record of R2.
- Found while running (not a choice): at exactly 17 weeks of history the user's own record has one origin, so R2 falls back to the series' spread as a single path, which the path bootstrap can only replay: a band of zero width (`recursion_check.md`, R2 at origin 17: inside 0.00). R3 replaces that record with the population record below 26 weeks.
