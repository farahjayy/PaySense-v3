# Plan — WP2 Budgets backend

> Execution: `refdocs/execution/2026-10-18-wp2-budgets.md`. Scope: work-packages.md WP2 (Topic A: budgeting).

## 1. Why this exists
Topic A asks for budgeting. Students need a monthly limit per spending category, to see how far through it they are (and whether the month's pace will break it), and a sensible starting point worked out from their own money rather than a blank form.

## 2. What's already covered
- One definition of "spent" (`flows.classify`, D-45) and the category breakdown (`domain/breakdown.py`).
- Engine 1's known flows for a month: confirmed recurring income and bills, unpaid instalments (`engines/forecasting/flows.known_flows`).
- Engine 1's split of discretionary spending from bills and plan payments (`engines/forecasting/series.py`).
- Category naming rules (D-43): built-in names spelled the app's way, own names case-insensitive.

## 3. Scope
**In:** table `budgets` (migration 0004), `db/budgets.py` on `ScopedTable` with fake support and an integration contract test; `domain/budgets.py` (`budget_status`, `suggest_budget`, category rules); the five routes from the WP-API contract; isolation and unauthenticated tests for each.
**Out:** budgets for income categories (Salary, Allowance) and the three that change the maths (Refund, Transfer, BNPL) — refused with a sentence; rollover of unused budget; weekly budgets.

## 4. Approach
- **Status** for a month = `category_breakdown` of that month (so totals equal the spending panel and the month summary), matched to budgets case-insensitively. `percent = spent / limit`; state `ok` below 80%, `near` from 80%, `over` from 100% (`ASSUMED` 20). For the current month, `projected = spent × days_in_month / day_of_month` and its own state; none for past or future months.
- **Suggestion** (plain arithmetic, no model): income = confirmed recurring income falling in the target month (`known_flows`), else the average income of up to three complete earlier months, else none; minus confirmed bills and unpaid instalments falling in the month (`known_flows`); minus a 10% buffer of income (`ASSUMED` 20) = the pool, never below zero. The pool is split by each category's share of **discretionary** spending in up to three complete earlier months — the same rows Engine 1 forecasts (not plan payments, not confirmed recurring items, not transfers or savings) — each limit rounded **down** to RM5 so the limits never add up to more than the pool. Every input is returned so the screen can show the working.
- To share Engine 1's discretionary rule rather than copy it, `series.py` exposes `covered_keys` and `classify_row` (behaviour unchanged; the series tests still pass).

## 5. Risks & unknowns
| Risk | Confidence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| A suggestion of RM0 everywhere for students already short of money | High for the personas | Say so in `notes`; the numbers are what the arithmetic gives |
| The 80% / 100% and 10% buffer choices | Medium | `ASSUMED` 20; constants in one place |
| Budget names drifting from category names | Low | Same normalisation as transactions (`locked_name`) and case-insensitive match |

## 6. Definition of done
Isolation, boundary (exactly 80% and 100%), empty-ledger, refund and BNPL-row tests pass; status totals equal the month summary on persona data; suggestion arithmetic tested by hand-computed cases; every route has a two-user test and an unauthenticated test.
