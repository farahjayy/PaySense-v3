## 1. Why this exists
The evaluation note and the outcome check found that (a) the forecast band is too narrow overall and far too narrow for short histories, and that clipping every simulated **week** at zero raises the simulated totals, which is the main cause of the income bias and of ASSUMED 40 (N1); (b) short histories have no mechanism that can reach 80% (W2, questions.md 22); (c) Engine 2 learns mostly the student's position before the purchase (R4) and its savings feature is backwards at zero income (R9, questions.md 34). This round builds the agreed "bundled v3" as a **candidate**, evaluates it against what is served, and switches what is served only where a rule written in this plan, before any fitting or scoring, says so.

## 2. Names used in this round

- **Band rule R2**: the served table (D-54), the user's own error record, weekly clip.

- **Engine 2 v2**: the served artefact (`models/engine2/v2`). **Engine 2 v3** (candidate): G5 + G1 on labels version 4.

## 4. Engine 1 (pre-registered)
### 4.1 Method
- **F1 — clip per month.** `simulate.simulate` takes `clip="week" | "month"`. With `month`, each path's weekly draws are summed into calendar months first (the same day weights as now) and the **monthly** spending and income totals are floored at zero; the point forecast stays floored per week. The weekly feature summaries (`weekly_spend_p50/p90`) keep a per-week floor at zero (a week of negative spending is not a week). The clip mode is a field of the band rule file, so it is covered by the digest Engine 2 already records.
- **F2 — population error record.** For a user with **fewer than 26 complete weeks** of history the error record is a pooled set of 13-week **ratio** error paths, `(actual − forecast) / level`, multiplied by the user's own level (mean of the last 26 weeks of the series, all of it if shorter). From 26 weeks the user's own record is used, as now. Not mixed. The population paths are fitted per series kind on group P (below): for each P student and each history length L in {17, 19, 21, 23, 25}, one seeded window end t in [L, 65] of the 78-week series, the served forecaster forecasts 13 weeks from `values[t−L:t]`; a window whose level is 0 is left out. Stored as `population_errors.json` (rounded to 4 decimals), named with its SHA-256 in the band rule file; the loader refuses a file whose digest differs, and Engine 2's artefact records it (D-40/D-55 style).
- **R3 refit.** On group D, per kind × history bucket (17, 26, 39, 52), the same rule as D-54: the smallest factor on the 1.0–6.0 grid (step 0.1) whose P10–P90 holds the realised 13-week total at least 80% of the time, else the factor with the best coverage (ties: the smallest); 300 draws, origins from week 17 every 4 weeks. Factors below 1 stay refused (`bands.parse_rule`).
- **Benchmark through the same bootstrap.** MA13 with its own population record (P), its own R3 table (D), scored on F.

### 4.2 Students (cohort indices; seeds are the Engine 1 evaluation seed 20251228 plus the offset shown)
| Group | Who | Use |
|---|---|---|
| D | the 300 band-fit students (`band_calibration.dev_profiles`, +1) | refit the R3 table |
| P | 300 drawn (+6) from indices 4,000–9,999 not divisible by 10, minus D, the 300 earlier fresh-check students, the 60 Engine 1 test students and the 2,000 `outcome_check.outcome_profiles()` | fit the population record |

### 4.3 Metrics (fresh check, F)
Per kind, overall and by bucket: share of origins whose realised 13-week total is inside the simulated P10–P90 (coverage), and the median of (simulated median − realised)/(recent level × 13) (median bias), for R1, R2, R3 and MA13-R3. A student-level paired bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed +8) gives 95% intervals for R3 − R2 coverage and |bias| differences; intervals are reported, the point estimates decide.

### 4.4 Serve rule for Engine 1 (all must hold, else R2 stays served)
1. For spending **and** income, overall coverage on F moves toward 0.80: |cov(R3) − 0.80| < |cov(R2) − 0.80|.
2. For spending **and** income, the median bias shrinks: |bias(R3)| < |bias(R2)|.
3. No kind × bucket cell loses more than 0.05 coverage: cov(R3) ≥ min(cov(R2) − 0.05, 0.80). *Refinement of the brief's rule, written before any run:* a cell that falls from above 0.80 to no lower than 0.80 has not lost calibration (it moved to nominal), so it does not count as a loss.
4. *Added, with the reason:* coverage of a 13-week total is a proxy; the product shows P(shortfall). On the outcome check (O, both cuts, all history lengths), the 95% student-bootstrap interval of Brier(LSTM, R3) − Brier(LSTM, R2) must not lie entirely above 0 (R3 must not be significantly worse at the number the product shows).

Engine 1 is decided before any Engine 2 label is made; the labels use whichever rule is then served.

### 4.5 Re-runs reported old and new
- `outcome_check`: served LSTM and MA13 under R2 and R3, constant; the month-so-far subgroups, especially "zero or positive".
- `recursion_check` keeps its point-forecast tables (F1/F2 do not change the point forecast) and gains a **band** section: at origins 17 and 26 on O, P10–P90 coverage and median bias of the simulated cumulative total at 13/26/39/52 weeks, R2 against R3 (300 draws).

## 5. Engine 2 (pre-registered)
### 5.1 Method
- **G5.** `savings_buffer_to_income` is replaced by **`savings_buffer_to_outgoings`**: the buffer divided by the expected monthly outgoings = existing monthly BNPL + debt-like bills + (52/12) × the simulated typical weekly discretionary spend (`disc_weekly_p50`). 0 with no buffer; the ratio cap (10) when outgoings are 0 and a buffer exists; capped at 10 otherwise. It is defined at zero income. Direction −1, last position kept. `DEFINITIONS_VERSION` v3; the schema hash changes, so only an artefact trained on it can serve.
- **G1.** The label is rewritten `p_with = b + (1 − b)·q` with b = `p_without` (simulated, shared seed) and q = P(short with the plan | not short without it), exact under the shared seed. Every candidate (logistic, LightGBM, XGBoost, random forest; D-05; the existing monotone directions) is trained on q by duplicate-and-weight with weight (1 − b) (rows with b = 1 carry no weight); every prediction and every score is on the composed p̂ = b + (1 − b)·q̂ against the label. Calibration (none / Platt / isotonic) is chosen on composed Brier of single-repeat out-of-fold predictions and fitted on q̂ with weight (1 − b). Served p = b_sim + (1 − b_sim)·q̂ with b_sim from the same request's simulation (already computed). Reasons are exact baseline Shapley values of the calibrated q̂ against the same reference student, multiplied by (1 − b_sim), so they sum to the purchase's added share against the reference and stay on the shown scale (threshold 0.02 unchanged). The API contract does not change.
- **Labels version 4:** 11,000 profiles (indices 0–10,999; `profile_seed` is index-stable), 1,000 paths, horizon to the last instalment (D-36), made with the Engine 1 rule served after §4.4.

### 5.2 Splits
- **Development:** index < 10,000 and index % 10 ≠ 0 (9,000 rows, as for v2). All choices are made here.
- **New lockbox:** indices 10,000–10,999. Excluded from everything; scored **once**, last, appended to `lockbox_log.jsonl`.
- **Old lockbox:** index < 10,000 and index % 10 = 0. Excluded from everything; scored once beside the new lockbox as **continuity only** (its third scoring after v1 and v2), disclosed.

### 5.3 Metrics and benchmarks (every table)
Brier (primary), log loss, AUC at 0.30, calibration slope, ECE, band agreement; benchmarks: the constant (development mean label), the logistic regression (same two-part target), **predict p_without** (the "no added risk" benchmark), and p_without + (1 − p_without)·(development mean q). New subgroup rows: band agreement where the purchase moves the band; added-risk error |p̂ − p|; baseline under 0.10. Stress tests on the new lockbox. Ablation and monotonicity cost as before.

### 5.4 Serve rule for Engine 2 (all must hold, else v2 stays served)
1. New-lockbox Brier skill 1 − B/B_const ≥ **0.9153** (v2's on its own lockbox, `versions.md`).
2. New-lockbox Brier below the predict-p_without benchmark's.
3. Serving check (200 fresh students, seed 90001, as for v2): the share where the shown band equals the direct simulation's band ≥ **0.900** (v2: 0.920, minus 2 points).

**Coupling (D-07).** Engine 2 v2's labels were made with R2 and its artefact refuses any other band rule (D-40). If R3 passed §4.4 but Engine 2 v3 fails §5.4, R3 is switched back off and both engines stay at the served versions; the merge notes record that R3 passed its own rule and is held back only by the coupling.

## 8. Risks
- Synthetic students only; the cohort holds total income while serving forecasts residual income (N2, F3 not done): income coverage measures a series that serving mostly places as known flows.
- A population record removes personalisation below 26 weeks (only the level is the user's).
- The two-part shown number carries the Monte Carlo noise of b (1,000 paths).
- Engine 2 still never sees a short history (N3, G4 not done).
